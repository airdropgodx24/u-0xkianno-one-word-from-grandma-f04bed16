## The word

**whatchamacallit**

It is the word Grandma texts about AI because it is the word she texts about every gadget she cannot name: warm, a little baffled, and funny all on its own. She does not know what the thing is called, she does not need to know, and the fifteen letters of cheerful surrender say more about her relationship with the machine than any technical term ever could. It is fresh, instantly readable, and unmistakably a grandmother giving up on the name mid-text.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (creating the directory if needed).
